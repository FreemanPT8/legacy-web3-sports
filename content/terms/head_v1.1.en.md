TERMS OF RESPONSIBILITY
HEAD OF HOUSE OF SPORT — LEGACY
Version 1.1 — January 2026

This is not a decorative role. It is a position of trust assumed before the entire Legacy community.

By accepting the role of Head of House of Sport in the Legacy ecosystem, I declare, consciously and responsibly, that:

1. I place the House's interests above personal interests
   - My role is to guide, clarify, and protect House members.
   - I will not use my position for commercial pressure, emotional manipulation, or misleading promotion.

2. I respect user autonomy
   - I acknowledge that no user is required to follow links, join projects, contact me directly, or join any initiative outside Legacy.

3. I communicate with clarity, truth, and responsibility
   - I commit to not making income promises, not guaranteeing results, not omitting risks, and not using misleading or ambiguous language.

4. I comply with platform operational limits
   - I fully accept communication frequency limits, mandatory use of approved templates, message audits, and anti-spam mechanisms with member feedback.

5. I act as a guardian of Legacy's reputation
   - I understand that any abuse of my position harms user trust, ecosystem integrity, and Legacy's global reputation.

6. I accept continuous evaluation and consequences
   - I acknowledge that my performance can be evaluated, abuse reports will be reviewed, and breaches of this term may result in immediate removal.

This commitment is assumed voluntarily, consciously, and aligned with Legacy values.
